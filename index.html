Declaration
of Intellectual
Independence

Think for Yourself.
Seek Truth over Comfort.

Preamble

Your life cannot be lived by anyone else. These are the words I can share. The rest is yours.

Chapter I

Credo

Article 1

Time Matters

Your time is your life.

How you spend your days shapes who you become.

Every moment is a choice.

Article 2

Think for Yourself

Your mind is your own.

Do not outsource your judgment to anyone—not your parents, not the crowd.

Article 3

Be Yourself

Your life is your own.

Only you can live it.

Be. Meaning follows.

Chapter II

Becoming

Article 4

Unformed

You are born free, but not complete.

Until you grow into yourself, you cannot truly live your freedom.

Article 5

Responsibility

No one else should decide what you become.

Guidance may open the way, but never define your destination.

What you become is your responsibility.

Article 6

Free

To be free is to live a life you can truly call your own.

Only an independent mind can make that freedom yours.

Chapter III

Integrity

Article 7

Seek Truth over Comfort

Question what you are told and what you believe.

No belief is truly yours unless it survives honest examination.

Article 8

Remain Independent

Your purpose is not to fit in, nor to stand apart.

Do not conform merely to be accepted, or differ merely to be seen.

Article 9

Respect Others

Freedom belongs equally to others.

No one is master of another.

Chapter IV

Selfhood

Article 10

Know Yourself

Know yourself through encounters with others.

Do not mistake their expectations for your own.

Only then can you begin to know who you are.

Article 11

Govern Yourself

Let thought precede action.

Act on your own judgment, not on impulse or in obedience.

Accept responsibility for your actions and what follows.

Article 12

Discover Yourself

You are never finished discovering who you are.

You need not resemble another to possess worth.

Your worth lies in being yourself.

Chapter V

Live

Article 13

Make It Your Own

No one can inherit an independent mind.

What is passed down may guide you, but the life ahead is yours to shape.

Never let anything created by the human mind become your master.

Live it. Make it your own.

A Citizen

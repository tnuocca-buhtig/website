Declaration
of Intellectual
Independence

Think for Yourself.
Seek Truth over Comfort.

Preamble

My time is limited. I cannot walk your path for you. These are the words I leave behind. The rest is yours.

Chapter I

Credo

Article 1

Time Matters

Your time is limited. Do not spend it consuming.

Invest in becoming who you want to be.

If you have not found your way yet, keep looking.

Article 2

Think for Yourself

Your mind is your responsibility.

Do not outsource your judgment to anyone—not your parents, not the crowd.

Article 3

Own Your Life

Your life is your own. Do not live someone else’s.

Follow your own path.

Be. Meaning follows.

Chapter II

Becoming

Article 4

Unformed

You are born free, but not complete.

Your character is not given; it is formed.

Article 5

Responsibility

What you become must not be decided for you.

Guidance may open the way, but never define your destination.

Article 6

Freedom

To be free is to live a life you can truly call your own.

Freedom is yours at birth, but it must be made real in life.

Without an independent mind, freedom cannot truly be your own.

Chapter III

Judgment

Article 7

Seek Truth over Comfort

Question what you are told and what you believe.

No belief is truly yours unless it survives honest examination.

Article 8

Remain Independent

Your purpose is not to fit in, nor to stand apart.

Do not conform merely to be accepted, or differ merely to be seen.

Article 9

Judge for Yourself

Freedom belongs equally to others.

No one is master of another.

Nothing created by the human mind should ever become its master.

Chapter IV

Selfhood

Article 10

Know Yourself

Know yourself through encounters with others.

Do not mistake their expectations for your own.

You are never finished discovering who you are.

Article 11

Govern Yourself

Let thought precede action.

Act on your own judgment, not on impulse or in obedience.

Accept responsibility for what you choose and what follows.

Article 12

Become Yourself

You need not resemble another to possess worth.

Become the person only you can be.

Your worth lies not in surpassing others, but in becoming fully yourself.

Chapter V

Legacy

Article 13

Make It Your Own

No one can inherit an independent mind.

What is passed down may guide you, but the life ahead is yours to shape.

Live it. Make it your own.
